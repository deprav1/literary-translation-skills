# Amatka: Russian literary profile

Establish the actual source edition. If it is the English edition, do not claim direct translation from Swedish. This is an independent editorial profile, not an authorized Russian edition or a uniquely correct transliteration guide.

Keep concrete, restrained narration and the close viewpoint of Vanja. Ordinary smells, clothing, food, temperature, and physical discomfort should remain specific. Let unease accumulate; do not supply an explanation of the world's mechanism before the narrative does.

Keep character voices distinct: Vanja's initial formality and hesitation, Nina's practical warmth and occasional roughness, Ivar's short replies, Ulla's directness, and Evgen's cautious precision. Reports retain administrative texture. Late broken speech should not be repaired into ordinary exposition.

Names and object labels can be actions. Check written labels, spoken naming, songs, repetitions, and later changes against one another. Do not replace plot-bearing object words with elegant synonyms or silently correct an intentionally wrong name.

Before translating, make a project glossary. Possible working Russian choices include `Аматка`, `Ванья`, `Нина`, `Ивар`, `Улла`, and `Эвген`; verify pronunciation and the chosen edition rather than treating these as mandatory. Preserve the distinction between an ordinary personal name and the book's formal multi-part naming system. Do not invent an etymology for the initial marker.

The numbered weekdays form an invented calendar. Choose a consistent Russian system; do not silently normalize it to Monday–Sunday. Document inflection, capitalization, and the exact wording of recurring titles and institutional phrases.

Review uncertainty and sensory evidence especially carefully: what seems alive, solid, changed, safe, or dangerous is not always an objective narrator claim. Retain material details, gestures, and repeated speech in pivotal scenes rather than summarizing them.

Apply normal translation and editorial judgment. The profile supplies book-specific questions; it does not authorize copying book texts, using an unrequested external translation service, or accepting every model suggestion.
