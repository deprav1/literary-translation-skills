Audit the Russian draft by source propositions: actor, action or state, object, negation, modality, number, chronology, location and direction, quotation speaker, and deliberately repeated information. Check whether each is actually represented, rather than merely suggested by a nearby noun. Do not require literal word matching: justified restructuring and necessary grammatical choices can preserve a proposition. Distinguish a demonstrated loss/addition from an uncertain reading or stylistic preference. Repair only supported discrepancies with the smallest natural change. Preserve the connected voice and recheck the whole affected sentence after each repair. Do not smooth correct prose merely to show activity. A no-change decision is valid.
