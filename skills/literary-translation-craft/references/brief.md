# Translation brief

Use only the fields useful for the current task. A short excerpt may need two sentences of context; a book needs a durable brief.

## Source and task

- Scope: excerpt / sample / full work.
- Source and target languages; original or intermediary text.
- Author, title, edition, genre, reader, and requested result.
- For files: source path, hash, and current edition. Use project-relative paths in shared records.

## Voice and form

- Narrator, focalization, distance, tense, and shifts.
- Sentence movement and rhythm; intentional plainness or intensity.
- Character voices, forms of address, hesitation, social registers.
- Recurring images, labels, refrains, names, and cultural references.
- Poetry/drama priorities: meaning, sound, rhyme, lineation, typography, performance.
- Permitted transformations and actual format constraints.

## Open questions

Record competing readings, missing context, and any external source still needed. A provisional choice is not a verified fact.
