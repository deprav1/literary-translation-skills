# Blank Space: Russian editorial profile

Establish the source edition and intended audience. Treat the source as a cultural argument, not a collection of uncontested facts. Preserve attribution, qualifiers, irony, and transitions between historical narrative and criticism.

Keep invention, popularity, commerce, status, and artistic value distinct. Suggested working terms:

| Source concept | Working Russian rendering |
|---|---|
| cultural invention | культурное новаторство |
| cultural stagnation | культурный застой |
| omnivorism | культурная всеядность |
| poptimism | поптимизм |
| counterculture | контркультура |
| counter-counterculture | контрконтркультура |
| artistic autonomy | автономия искусства |

A glossary entry is a choice for a meaning in context, not an instruction to replace every occurrence of an English string. Check unfamiliar concepts before treating them as ordinary words or expanding them into explanations.

Set a deliberate policy for book, album, and artwork titles. A known Russian title and an original bibliographic identifier can coexist. Keep quotations identifiable; do not imply that newly translated wording is an established published Russian quotation.

Record the source location and speaker for a contested cultural label or historical claim. Preserve the author's distinction between reporting a view and endorsing it; do not silently update facts or turn a provisional translation into a verified historical assertion.

Preserve dates, names, references, notes, and argumentative examples. A check of the beginning, middle, and end is useful sampling; it does not establish a complete semantic audit. Report the actual coverage and unresolved questions.

This profile contains original editorial guidance and short terminology notes, not the book or an official translation. Book-specific choices should not become universal rules for unrelated works.
